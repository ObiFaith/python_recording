# The Yellow Bus Odyssey: Escape from Third Mainland Bridge!

Welcome to **The Yellow Bus Odyssey: Escape from Third Mainland Bridge!**

You are a commuter inside a legendary Lagos Danfo (yellow commercial bus). It is 5:30 PM, the sun is blazing, and you are trapped in the mother of all gridlocks.

Your mission? **Survive the traffic, keep your sanity, and make it home.**

Copy and paste the game text below into your favorite document or shared space to play with friends, or just read through and choose your path!

---

## 🎬 Scene 1: The Stationary Danfo

You are wedged between a market woman carrying a basket of live chickens and a man wearing a heavy winter coat for reasons unknown. The driver is screaming at a conductor, and the engine is making a sound like a dying lawnmower. You haven't moved in 45 minutes.

- **Choice A:** Stay in the bus and pray for the traffic to move. *(Go to Scene 2)*
- **Choice B:** Paid your fare? Great. Jump out of the bus and look for an alternative. *(Go to Scene 3)*

---

## 🎬 Scene 2: The Chicken Uprising

You chose to stay. Suddenly, the bus jolts forward one inch. This startles the market woman's chickens. One particularly angry rooster escapes the basket and flies directly onto your head, pecking furiously at your hair.

The driver yells, "No fighting in my bus!"

- **Choice A:** Try to soothe the rooster by singing a popular Afrobeats song. *(Go to Scene 4)*
- **Choice B:** Grab the rooster and pass it out the window to a nearby Gala hawker. *(Go to Scene 5)*

---

## 🎬 Scene 3: The Tarmac Trek

You hit the asphalt. The heat radiating off the ground could roast plantains. As you walk past a line of stationary cars, a Lagos street vendor approaches you, aggressively waving a cold bottle of "Super-Mega-Ultra Drink" and a pack of plantain chips.

Your throat is parched.

- **Choice A:** Buy the drink and gulp it down instantly. *(Go to Scene 4)*
- **Choice B:** Ignore him and flag down an oncoming Okada (motorcycle) weaving through the cars. *(Go to Scene 5)*

---

## 🎬 Scene 4: The Gastro-Musical Trap

**If you sang to the rooster:** The rooster is a critic. It dislikes your pitch and pecks your forehead, causing you to dizzyingly tumble out of the emergency exit.

**If you bought the drink:** The drink was expired in 2022. Your stomach immediately lets out a roar louder than a generator.

You find yourself dazed at the foot of a pedestrian bridge. A mysterious man in a sharp suit offers you a shortcut through a "secret alleyway" to bypass the bridge entirely.

- **Choice A:** Trust the mysterious suit man and enter the dark alley. *(Go to Ending: The Bad Ending)*
- **Choice B:** Reject him, crawl up the pedestrian bridge steps, and push through the crowd. *(Go to Ending: The Good Ending)*

---

## 🎬 Scene 5: The Speed Demon

**If you threw the rooster:** The Gala hawker happily accepts the bird and gives you a free snack, but the conductor thinks you are stealing his luck and pushes you out of the bus.

**If you hopped on the Okada:** The driver introduces himself as "The Flash" and begins speeding at 120km/h between side mirrors.

Either way, you end up at the Toll Gate plaza. The traffic here is clearing, but a massive street party (Owambe) has spilled onto the road, blocking the final exit.

A high-society auntie in a massive Gele (headtie) mistakes you for a waiter and hands you a tray of jollof rice.

- **Choice A:** Drop the tray and run through the crowd toward the final bus stop. *(Go to Ending: The Bad Ending)*
- **Choice B:** Carry the tray, blend in, dance your way through the party, and exit out the back gate. *(Go to Ending: The Good Ending)*

---

## 🏆 Ending 1: The Good Ending (The Lagos Legend)

You made it!

Whether by dancing through an Owambe or conquering the pedestrian bridge, you break free from the chaos.

You find a clear road, hop into a smoothly moving cab, and arrive home in record time.

Your AC is working, your dinner is hot, and you have survived yet another day on the streets of Lagos.

**You win! 🎉**

---

## ❌ Ending 2: The Bad Ending (Forever in Traffic)

Oh no!

You fell into the classic Lagos traps. The alleyway leads you right into a never-ending maze of market stalls, or your attempt to flee the party causes a massive scene.

You get turned around and somehow end up exactly where you started—wedged right back inside a yellow Danfo, next to the exact same chickens.

The conductor looks at you and shouts:

**"Wole! Enter with your change!"**

**GAME OVER.**
